claude.ai, the desktop app and the mobile app

  1. 1Open Customize → Connectors.
  2. 2Press + and choose Add custom connector.
  3. 3Name it “Cyprus Tax Calculator” and paste the server address.

The Free plan allows one custom connector. On Team and Enterprise an owner adds it under Organization settings → Connectors first.

in a terminal

shell
claude mcp add --transport http cyprus-tax https://api.abasis.ai/mcp

CLI, IDE extension and app

shell
codex mcp add cyprus-tax --url https://api.abasis.ai/mcp

or in ~/.codex/config.toml:

[mcp_servers.cyprus-tax] url = "https://api.abasis.ai/mcp"

Any other client that speaks MCP over Streamable HTTP connects to the same address.

WebMCP in the browser

If your browser and AI agent support WebMCP, the agent can use tools directly on our pages. No server connection is required.

On the calculator →Four calculator tools and the question to our assistant. They include deductions, other income and employer costs without changing the visible calculator.On the 2025 return →The agent can read your draft, restore it and fill it in: first it previews the changes, then applies them after your confirmation. The figures appear in the visible form and are saved like your own typing.

It cannot submit the return or mark evidence as checked. You choose reliefs and fill in some fields yourself.

The draft never leaves your browser for our server. What a tool returns is seen by the agent and handled under its provider’s rules.

What reaches our server

  • Only what your assistant sends: income, deductions and payments for a calculation, or its question.
  • Calculations run on our server without being sent to AI. The result is returned to your assistant.
  • For a tax question, its text is sent to our AI provider to generate an answer.
  • We do not store the content of these requests, and there is no account to link them to.
  • Your draft stays in your browser. The server calculates a return only from data explicitly supplied by your assistant; WebMCP can work on the local draft.
