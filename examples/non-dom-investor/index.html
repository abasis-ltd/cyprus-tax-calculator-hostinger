The case

Someone who lives in Cyprus on investments: €80,000 of dividends and €10,000 of bank interest a year, no salary. Tax resident in Cyprus, but not domiciled here.

What to notice

  • Dividends and interest are exempt from income tax. For a non-domiciled resident they carry no defence contribution either; what remains is GESY at 2.65%, €2,385 a year.
  • The 2026 reform changes nothing here: the bands never reach this income, and the non-dom position already keeps it out of the defence contribution.
