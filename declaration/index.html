How to prepare your Cyprus tax return

Use this helper to prepare your 2025 TD.1 income tax return for filing in 2026. For salary planning under the 2026 rules, use the tax calculator.

  1. Import your employer’s IR63A certificate or enter the amounts manually.
  2. Add income and deductions missing from the certificate, using your supporting documents.
  3. Review the calculation and resolve the flagged issues before transferring figures to TAXISnet.
  4. Check the figures in the official portal and submit the return yourself.

Is this a 2025 or 2026 tax return?

The helper prepares the return for income earned in 2025, filed in 2026. The salary calculator separately compares the 2025 and 2026 tax rules.

What do I need to fill in the Cyprus TD.1 return?

Start with your employer’s IR63A emoluments certificate. Have records for your other income, tax withheld, contributions and any deductions you claim. You can also enter the figures manually.

Does this tool submit my return to the Tax Department?

No. It prepares figures and highlights items to review. You check the entries and submit your return yourself in the official portal.

Is the Cyprus tax return helper free?

Yes. Preparing the return in this helper is free and does not require an account.

Can I file the Cyprus return if my 2025 income is below €19,500?

Yes. The threshold only says who must file; a return below it is accepted, and people file one to have proof of Cyprus tax residence and income for a bank or a passport application. From tax year 2026 the obligation no longer depends on income for residents aged 25 to 70.

Before you press Final Submit in TAXISnet

  • Self-Assessment and Temporary Store are not a submission. The return counts as filed only after Final Submit, and the receipt number appears then.
  • After Temporary Store the error list disappears from the screen. Nothing has been fixed: press Self-Assessment again; the list is also behind the Total Errors button at the top of every page.
  • With employment code 1 the whole of section A2 must be empty. Dates or days left there produce three validation errors and block the calculation.
  • Refunds go only to the IBAN in your Tax Department record. If it has changed, update it through Tax For All with form T.D.2008 before you file; the return itself cannot change it.
  • Change to Temporary reopens a submitted return, but after the deadline the resubmission counts as late. Once the deadline has passed, ask the Tax Department before recalling anything.
  • Paying through a bank needs the reference from Statement of Due Amounts in the Tax Portal. Without it the payment may not be matched to the debt.
