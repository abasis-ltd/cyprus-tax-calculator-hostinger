Αν έχει ήδη καταβληθεί μέρος του τέλους, η επιβάρυνση εξαρτάται από το ανεξόφλητο ποσό· απευθυνθείτε στο Τμήμα Φορολογίας μέσω της ισχύουσας διαδικασίας. The calculation applies only when no stamp duty has been paid on the original. If part has already been paid, charges depend on the unpaid duty; ask the Tax Department to assess the balance. Αντίγραφα (Copies)

Εάν το τέλος χαρτοσήμου στο πρωτότυπο δεν υπερβαίνει τα €2, για κάθε αντίγραφο καταβάλλεται το ίδιο τέλος με το πρωτότυπο. Σε κάθε άλλη περίπτωση €2 ανά αντίγραφο.
If the duty on the original does not exceed €2, each copy pays the same duty as the original; otherwise €2 per copy. This rule is applied to the original duty before late charges in law mode. Tool mode preserves the Excel workbook's copy calculation.

Επιβαρύνσεις (Late stamping charges)

Εργαλείο Τμήματος (Tax Department tool, 2023 and 30.06.2025): up to 30 days after the contract: none. Later and under 6 months (by the tool's month count): duty ≤ €35 → +100%; duty > €35 → €35 + 10% of the excess. 6 months or more: double that. Duty is 1,5‰ / 2‰ of the exact amount.

Άρθρο 20 (text of the law): within 30 days of the first signature: none. Within six months: duty ≤ €2 → +€2; duty up to €35 → +100%; duty above €35 → €35 + 10 cents for every euro or part of a euro above €35. After six months: double those charges. Duty is €1,50 / €2,00 for every €1.000 or part of €1.000.

The Stamps-final-wp.xlsm workbook used for this replica retains the earlier calculation logic and includes the categories by parties, service provision agreements, and a cut-off after 31.12.2025 (Law 239(I)/2025).
